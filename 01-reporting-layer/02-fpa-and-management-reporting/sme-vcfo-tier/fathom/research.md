## Executive summary
- For a vCFO practice, Fathom is where the **monthly management pack** is produced: P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, KPIs, consolidation across a client's entities, and forecasts. The commentary has always been the slow, manual bit. That's where the AI landed.
- **Commentary Writer (launched March 2026; the What's New page dates it 12 Feb 2026, likely an early release)** drafts written commentary, insights and analysis from the report's financials, KPIs, charts and tables. Fathom calls it its biggest AI release. It's **included at no extra cost** for existing customers and trials. [1][2]
- Two design choices stand out: (1) the user sets **business context once** (goals, strategy, priorities, challenges) and adds **period notes** ("Q4 includes holiday sales spike"); (2) **"symbolic attribution"**: hover over any figure in the commentary to see the source data, calculation and logic behind it. [2]

## How the AI works — plain English

### Commentary Writer [2]
| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1. Business context (set once) | Goals, strategy, priorities, current challenges are stored and used in every report |
| 2. Period context | Notes for the month (launches, seasonality, hiring pauses) so identical variances aren't explained identically |
| 3. Analysis | Reads P&L, balance sheet, KPIs, charts and custom tables in the report |
| 4. Draft | Produces commentary like *"Revenue increased 8%, slightly below the 10% target due to delayed product launch in Q1"*, not just restating numbers |
| 5. Verify | **Symbolic attribution**: each figure links back to its source and calculation, so the advisor can "show their work" before it goes to the board |

### Other 2026 releases (non-AI but relevant to vCFO) [1]
- Consolidated group settings (Mar 2026), consolidated financials by division (Jun 2026), forecasting improvements (May 2026).

## Where it lands in the finance process
| Process | AI help | Human still owns |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly management pack | Drafted commentary with traceable figures | Final narrative, what to emphasise |
| Board reporting | Context-aware variance explanations | Advice and recommendations |
| Group / multi-entity reporting | (Consolidation engine; AI commentary on consolidated reports) | Eliminations, judgement |

## Governance & control considerations
- **Traceability is built in** (symbolic attribution), which is a good answer to "did the AI make that number up?". [2]
- Fathom hasn't published which LLM it uses or its data-processing region. Practices should check this against their **client confidentiality** obligations (CA ANZ Code of Ethics) before switching it on across a client base.
- Plan availability: the What's New page lists Commentary Writer under the **Pro plan** [1], while the launch blog says it's included at no extra cost [2]. Confirm for the practice's subscription.

## Presentation talking points (vCFO deck)
- **The commentary bottleneck is gone.** The vCFO's time moves from *writing* "revenue was up 8%" to *advising on what to do about it*.
- Business-context set-up is effectively **capturing the vCFO's knowledge of the client**. That context is now a practice asset.
- Pair with Spotlight AI and Xero/Syft analytics to show the SME reporting stack converging on AI-drafted packs.
