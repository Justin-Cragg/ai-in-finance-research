## Executive summary
- Spotlight is the reporting and forecasting toolkit for **advisory accountants and virtual CFOs**. It's designed to help practices move clients "from compliance to advisory".
- **July 2025: AI in the Executive Summary.** Spotlight AI drafts **highlights and recommendations** from the report data for up to five chosen topics, against any of 11 comparators (vs budget, YTD, projections…). It can also **rewrite existing commentary** shorter, longer or "more professional". [1][4]
- **April 2026: AI-Powered Action Plans.** Recommendations from the executive summary turn into **client-ready action plans** in seconds, closing the loop from "what happened" to "what we'll do". [2]

## How the AI works — plain English

### AI Suggestions [4]
| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Generate** | Pick topics and comparators; AI drafts highlights and recommendations for the Executive Summary or text panels |
| **Reformat** | Rewrite text shorter / longer / more professional |
| **Action Plans** | Recommendations are turned into an action plan automatically |

### Spotlight's own caveats [4]
- "AI can occasionally make mistakes", so users should verify before finalising.
- **AI text doesn't auto-update when data changes.** Only tagged and auto-text fields refresh. If the ledger is re-opened after commentary is drafted, regenerate or re-check it.

## Governance & control considerations
- Spotlight's documentation doesn't name the AI model or provider, or how data is handled. Ask before rolling out across a client base, given **client confidentiality** obligations.
- The "AI text doesn't refresh" behaviour is a real **quality-control risk** in pack production. Build a check into the review step. [4]
- A NZ vendor means local support and a NZ-relevant product roadmap, but a smaller AI R&D budget than global players.

## Presentation talking points (vCFO deck)
- **"From commentary to action plan in one click"**: the clearest SME example of AI moving from *explain* to *recommend*.
- NZ-built: a good local story for a NZ audience, and its SVCFO packaging shows vendors are designing for the virtual-CFO model.
- Pair with Fathom: both have converged on AI-drafted commentary. Differentiation is moving to **traceability** (Fathom) vs **actionability** (Spotlight).
