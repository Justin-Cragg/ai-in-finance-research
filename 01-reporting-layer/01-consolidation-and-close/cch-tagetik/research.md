## Executive summary
- CCH Tagetik is a **consolidation-first CPM suite** with very deep statutory logic: unlimited entities, multi-GAAP/IFRS, complex intercompany eliminations, currency translation, equity accounting. It's one of only three Gartner Leaders in close & consolidation in 2026, alongside Oracle and OneStream. [1][3]
- **Expert AI** is Wolters Kluwer's brand for "domain-expert" AI. A **Finance Brain** learns from customer data and workflows and applies financial logic, with agents across consolidation, planning, reporting and analytics. [2]
- **Global inTouch 2026 (May 2026)** showcased agentic AI: **Planning Sentinel** (agents that monitor performance, explain variances in narrative and simulate scenarios), plus specialised agents for **intercompany reconciliation, IFRS and ESG data entry, and regulatory data lineage**. All are human-in-the-loop. [5][6]

## How the AI works — plain English

### Expert AI building blocks [3]
| Capability | What it does |
|---|---|
| **AI transaction matching** | Automates intercompany reconciliation, the classic consolidation bottleneck |
| **AI anomaly detection** | Flags data-quality issues in submissions before they're consolidated |
| **AI auto-mapping** | Suggests chart-of-accounts mappings when loading subsidiary ledgers (useful in acquisitions) |
| **Ask AI** | Natural-language (text or **voice**) questions over financial data; returns visual answers [6] |
| **Intelligent Analytics** | Self-service reporting and dashboards with context-aware AI insights [6] |

### Agentic workflows (2026) [5][6]
- **Planning Sentinel**: always-on agents that watch performance, write variance narratives and run alternative scenarios.
- **Specialised finance agents**: intercompany reconciliation; IFRS and ESG data entry; regulatory data lineage (tracing a reported number back to source).
- Microsoft integration: an **Excel 365 add-in** and SharePoint integration put analytics where finance already works. [2]

### What finance leaders say they need (inTouch 2026 poll)
- **47%** of finance professionals named **trusted data** as the top prerequisite for adopting autonomous agents. Wolters Kluwer reports a persistent "readiness gap". [5]

## Where it lands in the finance process
| Process | AI help | Human still owns |
|---|---|---|
| Group consolidation | Intercompany matching, anomaly flags, auto-mapping of new entities | Eliminations judgement, top-side adjustments |
| Statutory & regulatory reporting | Data lineage agents, disclosure management | Disclosure judgement |
| ESG reporting | ESG data-entry agents; CSRD / ISSB / CBAM content | Materiality, assurance |
| Planning | Planning Sentinel monitoring and scenarios | Targets, assumptions |

## Governance & control considerations
- Data-lineage agents directly support **auditability of reported numbers**, a good answer to "where did this figure come from?"
- Thin NZ partner bench: implementation and support would likely come from Australia. Weigh this heavily for NZ-only groups.
- Pricing (third-party estimate): entry ~US$40–60k/yr, mid-market (20–50 entities) ~US$150–300k/yr; implementation often 1.5–2× annual licence. [3]

## Presentation talking points
- Use Tagetik to show that **all three Gartner consolidation Leaders** (Oracle, OneStream, Tagetik) now ship agentic AI. The consolidation engine is commoditising, and AI plus data trust are the differentiators.
- The **47% "trusted data first"** stat is a useful bridge to the Data layer story. [5]
