# Gemini (Google)

*Layer: General AI · Last researched: 5 Oct 2026*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Vendor** | Google |
| **Products** | Gemini app, **Gemini in Google Workspace** (Sheets, Docs, Slides, Gmail), **Gemini Enterprise** (agent platform), **Gemini Enterprise for Financial Services** (preview) |
| **AI maturity** | **Stage 3→4** – agent platform with MCP connectors and A2A (agent-to-agent) APIs; finance offering in preview |
| **NZ relevance** | Relevant mainly for Google Workspace organisations (smaller share of NZ corporates than Microsoft 365). Note: Gemini in BigQuery processes Oceania data globally (US/EU-only residency) [3] |

## Executive summary
- For finance teams on **Google Workspace**, Gemini is built into Sheets/Docs/Slides at no extra seat (Workspace AI bundling) — formula help, analysis, summarising, drafting.
- **Gemini Enterprise for Financial Services (preview, 25 Aug 2026)**: purpose-built financial skills, secure MCP connectors, a **Financial Research Agent** with 50+ skills and explainability (confidence scores, methodology, data snapshots, citations), plus an SI/partner ecosystem that **includes Deloitte**. [1]
- Skills are aimed at credit, KYC, portfolio and research — **no dedicated close/reconciliation/FP&A skills** yet. [1]

## How the AI works — plain English
| Capability | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Gemini in Sheets/Docs/Slides** | Generate formulas, analyse tables, summarise, draft narrative, build slides [2] |
| **Gemini Enterprise** | Front door for agents (Google-built, partner and custom), connected to company data |
| **Financial Research Agent** | 50+ skills; shows confidence, method and sources; callable by other agents via A2A [1] |
| **MCP connectors** | Role-based access to financial platforms and licensed data; Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace connectors [1] |
| **Data agents (BigQuery/Looker)** | See Data layer → Google BigQuery & Looker |

## Governance considerations
- Explainability features (confidence scores, methodology) are a useful model for finance AI review. [1]
- Residency limitations for Oceania on some Gemini data services. [3]

## Presentation talking points
- **Deloitte is a named launch partner** for Gemini Enterprise for Financial Services — relevant positioning point. [1]
- Use Gemini's "confidence score + methodology" design to discuss what auditors should expect from AI outputs.
